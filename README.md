### AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner

Learn the foundations of the AWS Cloud, including why organizations use cloud services, how security responsibilities are shared, and how common services support business needs. The workshop also covers pricing, cost management, support options, and exam preparation.

### AWS Certified AI Practitioner

Explore the foundations of artificial intelligence and machine learning, including generative AI and its practical uses. Learn how to choose suitable approaches for a business task, assess the quality of results, and consider responsible use, privacy, security, and oversight.

## How you will learn

- Follow the slides and diagrams to build your understanding step by step.
- Use the quizzes to check what you have learned, then review the answer explanations.
- Resume your learning with progress saved in the browser you use.
- Review printable study materials, with the option to include diagrams, quizzes, and answers.
- Explore optional practice labs through AWS Skill Builder. Some labs require separate access.
